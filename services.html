What We Do

Vertical denim production, start to finish

Every stage of the garment's life — fibre, fabric, wash and finish — is handled inside Soorty's own mills and factories.

Spinning

We spin our own yarn from raw and organic cotton, giving us control over quality and consistency from the very first fibre.

Weaving & Fabric Design

Our denim fabric mills weave and design denim in-house, engineered for durability, comfort stretch and finish.

Dyeing & Laundry

Technologically advanced laundries — home to Pakistan's first Tonello machines — shape every wash, from raw indigo to distressed vintage finishes.

Garment Manufacturing

Cut-to-pack garment factories turn finished fabric into ready-to-ship jeans and denim apparel, produced to international retail standards.

ProSeam Thread Dyeing

Our in-house sewing thread facility produces up to 180 tons of dyed thread a month, including Cradle to Cradle certified organic and recycled options.

Sustainable Solutions

From the Soorty Organic Cotton Initiative to LEED Platinum and Cradle to Cradle certified facilities, sustainability runs through every service we offer.

Order Management

From brief to delivery

Our vertical setup lets us manage an order end-to-end — design, sourcing, production, laundry and finishing — with the order management and logistics to deliver reliably to fashion partners around the world.
