Who We Are

Denim, made whole

What began in the early 1980s as a small garment tailoring shop in Karachi has grown into a fully vertical denim powerhouse — spinning our own yarn, weaving and dyeing our own fabric, and finishing garments in our own laundries. That control over every stage is what lets us hold the line on quality, speed and sustainability at the same time.

Today Soorty produces denim fabric and garments for some of the biggest names in global fashion, with research labs, design studios and offices spanning the United States, Turkey, the Netherlands, Spain and Bangladesh.
