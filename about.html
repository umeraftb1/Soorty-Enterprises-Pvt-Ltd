About Soorty

From a tailoring shop to a global denim house

Four decades of vertical integration, sustainability and craftsmanship — built in Karachi, worn worldwide.

Our Story

Established 1983

Soorty started in the early 1980s as a small apparel manufacturing and tailoring operation in Karachi. In the 1990s, we built a state-of-the-art denim laundry — home to the first Tonello machines in Pakistan — which laid the foundation for the country's modern denim industry.

Since then, Soorty has grown into Pakistan's largest vertically integrated denim company: spinning yarn, weaving and dyeing fabric, running our own laundries, and manufacturing garments, all under one roof. We now rank among the top five clothing producers in Pakistan and one of Asia's fastest-growing denim fabric companies.

Our footprint has expanded beyond Pakistan into Bangladesh, with research labs, design studios and offices in the United States, Turkey, the Netherlands and Spain — keeping us close to the markets and trends we serve.

Our Vision

To lead sustainable denim production through research, innovation and consistently high-quality products — using denim as a force for good in the communities where we work.

Doing It Right

Sustainability, built into the mill

LEED & Cradle to Cradle

Our denim mill and garment factory hold LEED Platinum status, with Cradle to Cradle Certified Gold-rated denim fabric — a vertical solution across fabric and garment alike.

Soorty Organic Cotton Initiative

SOCI supports around 1,300 farmers growing organic cotton, building capacity for sustainable farming and strengthening livelihoods through financial inclusion.
